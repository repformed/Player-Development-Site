Membership and billing

Cancel future renewals. Keep the time already paid for.

You can cancel anytime to stop future renewals. Access continues through the end of the paid period, including an annual paid period. Your last access date should be shown clearly; canceling a renewal is separate from requesting a refund.

Refund requests are reviewed individually.

We plan to review requests case by case, distinguishing a change of mind from a duplicate charge or missing service. No automatic refund amount or fixed request window has been set. This approach does not remove applicable consumer rights.

Prices are in USD per athlete. Each higher level includes the lower levels’ proposed benefits. Read the package comparison and proposed prices; this page is not a checkout or an active cancellation tool.

Elite Coaching

Two reviews each month, not a year’s credits upfront.

Each subscribed Elite athlete would receive two drill corrections per month, on monthly and annual plans. Each submitted clip can be up to three minutes. Ordinary unused credits expire at the monthly reset; they do not carry over.

The monthly allowance resets on the join-date anniversary. If that date does not exist in a shorter month, use that month’s last day. An annual plan does not receive 24 credits upfront. The exact reset clock time will be published before enrollment opens.

A three-business-day target, by 5 p.m. Central.

The target is 5 p.m. on the third business day, using America/Chicago time and its daylight-saving changes. The arrival day does not count. Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding a holiday list published in advance.

A weekend or holiday arrival is treated as arriving on the next business day; counting starts after that treated arrival day. This is a target, not a guaranteed delivery time.

Monday arrival

Assuming no listed holidays: Tuesday is day one, Wednesday day two and Thursday day three. The target is Thursday at 5 p.m. Central.

Saturday arrival

Assuming no listed holidays: treat Monday as the arrival day. Tuesday is day one, Wednesday day two and Thursday day three. The target is Thursday at 5 p.m. Central.

No monthly group film-study or group coaching session is included in the proposed package promises.

When the target is missed

A notice, a returned credit and a new date.

If a review is late, the planned response is to notify you, return the review credit and offer a new delivery date. If you decline that date, or the new date is also missed, an owner reviews a fair refund or another remedy.

A returned late-review credit remains usable for 30 days after it is issued, even if the membership ends, without requiring another payment. It applies to the owed review; it does not extend access to the whole Elite plan. Accepting delivery of an already-owed review does not require renewal.

Restarting a subscription does not create duplicate late-review credits or restart an existing remedy credit’s 30-day clock.

Families and support

Parents control permission, billing and recovery.

The planned family design uses a parent’s own email and a separate athlete profile for each child. A child could have a limited username/password login without a child email; they would not share the parent password or receive billing access. The separate adult pathway is for people aged 18 or older.

All child and teen participation remains paused pending professional review and tested consent, payment, privacy and safety processes. The verification method and these processes must be reviewed and tested before minor participation opens. Reading a public lesson does not activate an athlete account.

Both owners share the support inbox.

Devyn and Victor both retain visibility. Whoever takes a question marks it as theirs to avoid duplicate replies. The ordinary acknowledgment target is one business day within published working hours. Urgent safety or privacy concerns are intended for both owners.

Exact staffed hours have not yet been published. Contact the team for questions; expressing interest is not enrollment.

Personal media and inactive profiles

Different records have different clocks.

30 DAYS

Your submitted original clip

The planned original-clip limit is 30 days after the coach review is delivered. This means the athlete’s submitted video, not Repformed’s training library.

90 DAYS

The finished coach review

The planned finished-review limit is 90 days after delivery. Cancellation or restart does not reset either personal-media clock.

The finished-review window is separate from your submitted clip’s window. These are planned retention limits; automatic deletion is not currently operating.

12 calendar months after paid access ends.

The planned inactive-profile window preserves the profile, training progress and saved-drill references for 12 calendar months after paid access ends. Paid reactivation during that window stops the pending inactivity expiry. Logging in alone is not paid reactivation.

We plan to warn you before inactivity deletion. The advance-warning interval has not been set. A requested account deletion is handled separately, subject to the final privacy policy and required records.

Final handling of record exceptions, backups, deletion requests and abandoned or unreviewed clips still needs privacy review. Automated deletion is not active.
